# **Data Visualization Trainee at Excelerate**

## 📌 Overview  
My **Early internship experience at Excelerate**, where I served as both **Data Analyst** and **Project Manager**.  
The internship focused on analyzing and optimizing **Facebook advertising campaigns**, to identify underperforming segments, reallocating budgets, and delivering data-driven recommendations.  

---

## Internship Role  
- **Organization:** GlobalShala  
- **Role:** Project Manager & Data Analyst  
- **Objective:** Evaluate Facebook ad campaign performance, design data visualizations, and present actionable insights to stakeholders.  
- **Focus Areas:**  
  - Data analysis of campaign performance metrics (CPR, CPC, CTR, ULC) in a team.  
  - Visualization design to simplify insights for decision-makers.  
  - Project management to ensure smooth collaboration and timely delivery.
